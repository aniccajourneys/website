Plan your journey

Bali, Komodo and the cliffs

7 nights, private throughout. From around £8,000 per couple, excluding international flights.

  1. Nights 1–2

    Ubud

    A jungle villa with its own pool. Your driver takes you through the rice terraces and to waterfalls, then a spa afternoon for two.

  2. Nights 3–4

    Komodo by boat

    A short flight east, then aboard a traditional phinisi. Sunrise on Padar, the dragons of Komodo, swimming with manta rays, Pink Beach.

  3. Night 5

    Labuan Bajo

    Back on land at a resort above the bay, with a private dinner as the sun goes down over the islands.

  4. Nights 6–7

    Uluwatu

    A clifftop villa on Bali's south coast to slow right down before you fly home.

Questions couples ask

When is the best time to go?

April to October is the dry season, with calm seas around Komodo and clear skies in Bali. We can plan outside those months too and will be honest about the trade-offs.

Can we change the itinerary?

Yes. The route above is a starting point. We can add nights, swap hotels, include a proposal or celebration, or add Sumba at the end.

How does payment work?

A deposit secures your trip and the balance is due before you travel. The exact amounts and dates are set out in writing in your proposal before you pay anything.

What if our plans change?

Cancellation and change terms are written into your proposal, so you know where you stand before booking. We strongly recommend travel insurance from the moment you book.

Who looks after us on the ground?

Licensed local partners in Indonesia run the drivers, guides, boat and hotels. You also have a direct WhatsApp line to us for anything at all.

Do you book flights?

We include the domestic flight to and from Komodo. International flights are not included, so you can fly from wherever suits you and use your own airline points.
