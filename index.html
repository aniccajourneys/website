Plan your journey

Bali, Komodo and the cliffs

7 nights, private throughout. From around £8,000 per couple, excluding international flights.

  1. Nights 1–2

    Ubud

    A jungle villa with its own pool. Your driver takes you through the rice terraces and to waterfalls, then a spa afternoon for two.

  2. Nights 3–4

    Komodo by boat

    A short flight east, then aboard a traditional phinisi. Sunrise on Padar, the dragons of Komodo, swimming with manta rays, Pink Beach.

  3. Night 5

    Labuan Bajo

    Back on land at a resort above the bay, with a private dinner as the sun goes down over the islands.

  4. Nights 6–7

    Uluwatu

    A clifftop villa on Bali's south coast to slow right down before you fly home.

Included

  • Hand-picked villas, resort and boat cabin
  • A private driver and English-speaking guides
  • Domestic flight to and from Komodo
  • National park fees and the activities above
  • A direct WhatsApp line to us throughout

Why Anicca

Anicca is an old word for impermanence: the idea that nothing stays the same. We think that's the best reason to go somewhere extraordinary with the person you love, while the moment is yours.

I'm Marius, the founder. Before a route goes on sale I travel it myself, and every trip is run on the ground by licensed local partners we've vetted. You deal with one person from first call to flight home.
